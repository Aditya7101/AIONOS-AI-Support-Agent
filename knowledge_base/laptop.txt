AIONOS LAPTOP TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Problem:
Employee's laptop is slow, freezing, or behaving unexpectedly.

Step 1:
Close unnecessary applications.

Step 2:
Restart the laptop.

Step 3:
Check available storage space.

Step 4:
Install pending operating system updates if available.

Step 5:
If the problem continues, create an IT support ticket.

Priority:
Repeated freezing or major performance problems are Medium priority.

Escalation:
If the laptop cannot boot or the employee cannot perform their work, escalate to IT support.
