AIONOS WIFI TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Problem:
Employee cannot connect to company Wi-Fi.

Step 1:
Check that Wi-Fi is enabled on the device.

Step 2:
Disconnect from the Wi-Fi network and reconnect.

Step 3:
Check whether other devices can connect to the same network.

Step 4:
Restart the computer.

Step 5:
If the issue continues, create an IT support ticket.

Priority:
Normal Wi-Fi connectivity problems are Medium priority.

Escalation:
If multiple employees are affected by the same Wi-Fi problem, escalate to the IT infrastructure team.
