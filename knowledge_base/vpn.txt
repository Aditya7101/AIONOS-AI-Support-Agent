AIONOS VPN TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Problem:
Employee cannot connect to the company VPN.

Step 1:
Check that the employee has a working internet connection. Open a normal website to confirm internet access.

Step 2:
Check multi-factor authentication. Make sure the employee approves the MFA notification or enters the correct authentication code.

Step 3:
Close the VPN application completely and reopen it.

Step 4:
Restart the computer and try connecting to the VPN again.

Step 5:
If the VPN still does not work after these steps, create an IT support ticket.

Priority:
Normal VPN connectivity issues are Medium priority.

Escalation:
If the employee cannot access critical company systems because of the VPN problem, escalate the issue to the IT support team.
